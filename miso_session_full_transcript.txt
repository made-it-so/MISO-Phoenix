The strongest real differentiator. MISO runs entirely on-premise — no data leaves your infrastructure, no OpenAI dependency, no per-token cost at inference time. Generic RAG over a document dump is a commodity. MISO's design intent — auditing every ingested node for signal quality, assigning scores, building linked axioms — is an attempt to build a **high-density, low-noise knowledge base** rather than a document retrieval system. If executed well, the substrate becomes proprietary. Nobody else has your specific graph of verified axioms.

### 3. Auditability / Glass Box Derivation
The `miso_engine.py` "prove_claims" pattern — showing which source nodes grounded a conclusion — is genuinely valuable in enterprise contexts where "the AI said so" is not acceptable. Traceable reasoning chains are a real differentiator vs. black-box LLM APIs.

### 4. Cost Structure at Scale
Local inference has zero marginal cost per query. At scale (high query volume, multi-tenant), this becomes a massive structural advantage over API-dependent competitors.

| Moat | Strength | Honest Assessment |
